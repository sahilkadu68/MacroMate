# Business Model Hypotheses

**Status:** hypotheses for validation, not established market facts. No customer interviews, conversion data, paid users, or operating-cost measurements are available.

## Segment and value proposition

Initial segment: Indian college students and beginner gym-goers who manage food spending and want practical nutrition feedback. Proposed value: quickly compare attainable meals by estimated cost and nutrition, correct prices locally, log what they ate, and see how spending relates to self-set goals.

## Free and possible paid value

- **Free hypothesis:** local food logging, basic target tracking, price correction and basic daily suggestions. Device-local account deletion exists; data export remains unimplemented.
- **Potential premium hypothesis:** multi-day constrained planning, reusable plans/lists, scenario comparisons, and deeper historical budget/nutrition review.
- Never paywall access to personal records, safety information, correction, export, or deletion.
- No subscription SDK, entitlement service, store product, or transaction verification is implemented. Do not display simulated purchase as real billing.

## Pricing hypotheses (INR)

Test concepts rather than announce prices: ₹49–99/month and ₹399–699/year for planning convenience. These are unvalidated interview/prototype test ranges, not competitor-backed market rates or recommendations. Test monthly, annual, and one-time/student-access alternatives with actual students and explicit trade-offs.

## Competitor evidence

Healthify advertises macro/fibre logging and coaching/AI features ([official app page](https://www.healthifyme.com/app/), checked 2026-10-28). MyFitnessPal documents meal planning and grocery-list features in Premium+ ([official help page](https://support.myfitnesspal.com/hc/en-us/articles/34889191368077-The-difference-between-Free-Premium-and-Premium), checked 2026-10-28). Cronometer describes differentiated food sources and reviewed entries ([official source notes](https://support.cronometer.com/hc/en-us/articles/360018239472-Data-Sources), checked 2026-10-28). These establish category examples, not India conversion rates, demand, or MacroMate pricing.

## Cost model to measure

Current local app has no configured backend or subscription fees. A future service cost model should measure: database/auth hosting, backups, storage/egress, email/SMS authentication, analytics (if consented), support, app-store fees, payment taxes/fees, and engineering/maintenance time. No provider quote or usage load assumption is available; therefore no credible rupee operating-cost or profit estimate is reported.

## Acquisition and retention hypotheses

Potential low-cost channels to test: campus fitness groups, student clubs, peer demos, and helpful public meal-planning content. Retention should come from reduced decision/logging effort, current local prices, useful repeat plans and progress review—not streak pressure or inflated claims. Obtain consent before campus research or marketing.

## Validation plan

First test task usefulness with 5–8 exploratory interviews and observed prototype sessions. Then test actual price corrections, repeat use over 2–4 weeks, and willingness to pay with explicit price cards. Track task success and reasons for abandonment. Do not report market size, revenue forecasts, profitability, or validated demand without reliable data and documented assumptions.
